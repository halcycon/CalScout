Your calendars

Thunderbird handles events, reminders and synchronisation. Missing calendars stay registered, preserving local data.

Connect an account

Existing secure CalDAV accounts are included automatically. For a new account, start with your email address. Thunderbird will ask for a calendar password if needed.

Advanced server settings

CalScout never reads mail passwords. Calendar credentials are managed by Thunderbird.

Discovery preferences

Disabling preserves subscriptions and local data. Re-enable them in Thunderbird after access returns.

Diagnostics

Structured local log. No passwords, tokens or authentication headers are recorded.
